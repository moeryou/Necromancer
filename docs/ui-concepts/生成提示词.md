# UI 概念图生成提示词

工具：内置 imagegen。输入图：用户提供的墓园战斗地图，用作背景与构图参考。三版均使用共同规范，并分别附加方向提示词。

## 共同规范

Use case: ui-mockup, compositing.
Create a polished actual in-game landscape HUD concept screenshot for the Chinese 2D game 梦想成为亡灵大师. Input image is the exact battlefield background and composition anchor. Preserve its purple-brown graveyard occupying the left 73%, the yellow-green wilderness occupying the right 27%, and the vertical fence with its central gap at x73% and y50%. Preserve background artwork and lighting, do not replace it with another map. Output one 16:9 full-screen screenshot, not a poster, collage, multi-panel, device frame, or title screen.
Style: cute Western cartoon, clean cel shaded flat fills, rounded friendly proportions, consistent medium dark-plum outlines, one hard-edged shadow tone, soft low-saturation palette. No photorealism, 3D renders, metal sci-fi HUD, neon, huge bloom or black gothic horror.
Gameplay scene identical in all variants: a small cute purple-hooded necromancer with ivory face and teal magic shield exactly at screen center x50% y50%. Four modest 2x2-footprint facilities on the left and lower-left, some ivory skeleton soldiers and two floating mint ghosts, one skeleton archer behind melee troops. A small bone arrow tower on the upper side of the approach corridor. A few cute human militia enter from the right wilderness through the fence gap, continue toward the central hero. Preserve broad open space around the central hero and gate, do not cover them with HUD. Buildings are small readable game sprites, never oversized illustrations.
UI must explain mana equals shield: only ONE shared mana/shield meter labeled exactly "魔力 / 护罩", value "380 / 500", recovery "+10/秒". Bone counter "120", forged bone counter "8", wave "4 / 6", next wave "28秒", army "8 / 24". No coins, gems, stamina, separate health bar or unrelated monetization.
Building controls five distinct cards/icons with exact short labels and costs:
"幽魂祭坛" "200"; "骷髅墓穴" "250 + 30"; "骨弓塔巢" "300 + 50"; "骨箭塔" "200 + 40"; "锻骨坊" "150".
Use mana droplet and bone icons to identify cost units. "骨箭塔" selected.
One skill control "枯萎之咒" with mana cost "80" and pause icon at top right.
Show localized faint grid only near a selected tower placement ghost in the upper approach zone, mint route dots bending around two buildings toward hero, and a small orange callout "堵路后敌人会拆建筑" beside that placement, not blocking the hero or gate. One damaged building has a short blood-orange health bar; healthy buildings do not have health bars.
Typography bold rounded Chinese sans-serif, clean readable lettering, short text only. HUD compact with clear icon-first hierarchy and good contrast, total opaque HUD area at most ~20%; preserve landscape battlefield and generous combat space. No decorative giant headings or title text inside image. This is concept art, not engine implementation.

## A：幽灵糖果

Direction A — Ghost Candy / 幽灵糖果. Friendly compact mobile tower-defense HUD. Cream-lilac panels, soft mint selected states, dusty purple borders, tiny cheerful ghost and bone motifs, coral danger accents. Top-left two small rounded resource counters; top-center a generously readable horizontal mana/shield bar with a little cute ghost medallion, x35-65%; top-right a compact wave pill and pause. Bottom a low rounded floating lavender tray spanning x10-78% with five evenly sized cream building cards, chunky cel shaded building icons, labels and costs. Selected bone tower mint outline with raised hard shadow. Skill is a large mint circular button at bottom-right x89% with small attached cost badge. All panels visually soft and plush but flat shaded, NO candy food, no glossy plastic or gradients. Most immediately accessible and cute of the three directions.

## B：墓园手账

Direction B — Graveyard Field Journal / 墓园手账. A wandering necromancer's practical illustrated spellbook HUD, cute storybook craft, pale oat parchment #F3E4C8, muted grape covers, sage bookmarks, dusty pumpkin accents, dark cocoa lettering. Top-center ONE parchment-framed horizontal mana/shield bar with violet ink-like flat fill; top-left two small paper tags for resources, top-right folded page wave banner and pause. Bottom-left/lower-middle x3-74% is a compact shallow OPEN SPELLBOOK as build menu: five clear illustrated building stamps/cards across two pages, purple tab selected bone tower, readable labels and costs, understated drawn spine, small bookmark category tabs. Book occupies only bottom 15% height, not half the battlefield. Lower-right round wax-seal curse button with sage magic emblem, cost80. One small parchment tooltip for block-road warning. Crisp cel-shaded surfaces with no dense grunge, no photorealistic paper, no elaborate fantasy serif Chinese letters. More narrative, handmade and cozy than A, but equally usable.

Diegetic graveyard management toolkit UI, weathered lavender stone plaques, ivory rounded bone brackets, pale sage-painted wood, desaturated peach accents; cute flat cel shading, very light stylized grain, not realistic material. Top-center mana/shield as an ivory-bone framed purple horizontal strip with a small rounded skull charm; top-left resources on a low sage wooden sign; top-right wave engraved on a small rounded purple gravestone plaque, pause beside it. Unlike A and B, building controls are a compact VERTICAL five-slot toolbar along far LEFT x1-11%, y24-86%, with small icons plus readable labels and costs, selected bone tower mint inset. Bottom-left-to-middle x14-53% a shallow contextual wooden plate reading "骨箭塔", costs "200 + 40" and button "建造", approximate only 9% screen height. Bottom-right an ivory-rimmed sage curse rune disc and cost80. Keep hero and fence gap completely clear. Asymmetric compact HUD emphasizes usable world space, camp facilities and tactical placement. Do not make scary skulls, brown muddy dark panels, oversized tombstones, or decorative full-screen frame.
